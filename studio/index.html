Horn

The page carries on behind the dock. Open the studio, mute the cello, keep reading — that is the point of putting it down there rather than on a page of its own.

Tone colour

Conical bore, expanding gradually from mouthpiece to bell — mellow and round where trumpets and trombones are bright.

Blends

Cello in unison, woodwinds, strings. The horn glues a woodwind chord together better than anything else in the brass.

Limits

Quiet playing in the upper register is genuinely difficult. Eight horns in unison will bury the orchestra.
